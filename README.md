## Research

**Learned control of hybrid battery storage.** Reinforcement-learning and rule-based dispatch for hybrid
packs (lead-acid, LTO, NCA) behind DC-DC converters, serving GPU data-centre loads under published utility
tariffs: peak shaving, monthly demand charges, and degradation-aware operation. Carried out with
Sandia National Laboratories.

**Aqueous zinc batteries.** Mildly acidic Zn–MnO₂ chemistry and acetate-based electrolytes with a wider
stability window, aimed at grid-scale storage.

**Before the PhD.** Heterogeneous catalysis, biodiesel, and photocatalytic hydrogen production
(M.Sc., Sharif University of Technology).

## The control work, in three stages

| Stage | Question | Load data |
|---|---|---|
| Electric vehicles | How should power be split between a high-energy and a high-power pack over a drive cycle? | FTP-75, US06, WLTC |
| Islanded data centre | Can a learned split keep both packs inside their current limits with no grid behind them? | MIT SuperCloud GPU cluster |
| Grid-connected data centre | How much of the monthly billed demand can the packs remove under a real tariff, and how close does a learned policy get to the bound? | MIT SuperCloud, NREL Kestrel GenAI |

The code is private while the papers are in preparation. Collaborators and reviewers can request access.
